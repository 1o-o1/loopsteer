Q: Which factor will most likely cause a person to develop a fever? Answer Choices: (a) a leg muscle relaxing after exercise (b) a bacterial population in the bloodstream (c) several viral particles on the skin (d) carbohydrates being digested in the stomach
A: The answer must be something that causes the body to raise its temperature. Of the above choices, only a bacterial population in the bloodstream is an infection the body fights with a fever. So the answer is (b).

Q: A student riding a bicycle observes that it moves faster on a smooth road than on a rough road. This happens because the smooth road has Answer Choices: (a) less gravity (b) less friction (c) more gravity (d) more friction
A: The answer must explain why the bicycle moves faster on a smooth surface. Of the above choices, a smooth road has less friction, so less of the rider's effort is lost. So the answer is (b).

Q: Which of these is the best conductor of electricity? Answer Choices: (a) a wooden spoon (b) a plastic ruler (c) a copper wire (d) a glass rod
A: The answer must be a material that lets electric current pass easily. Of the above choices, only copper is a metal, and metals conduct electricity well. So the answer is (c).
